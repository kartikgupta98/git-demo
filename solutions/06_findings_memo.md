# Customer and Revenue Health Report: Findings

**To:** Leadership team
**From:** Data analyst
**Data:** every invoice line, 1 December 2009 to 9 December 2011 (Online Retail II, UCI)

## The headline

The business took **£18.98 million** in net revenue over two years, from **39,516 orders** and **5,852 identified customers**, at about **£480 per order**. Like for like, it is not growing: January to November 2011 was only **2.2%** above the same months of 2010.

## Five findings

1. **13.5% of revenue has no customer attached.** £2.57 million of sales sit on invoices with no customer ID. That is the second-biggest "market" after the UK, and we can't segment it, retain it or contact it.
2. **A few customers carry whole countries.** One customer is 95.8% of all Netherlands revenue, one is 85.7% of Australia's, and one is 52.5% of Ireland's (EIRE). Losing any one of them would wipe out most of that market.
3. **Revenue is seasonal and peaks in November.** Sales climb from September and peak in November in both years (£1.43 million in November 2011). April 2011 was 24% below April 2010, which needs explaining.
4. **Loyal customers are the business.** Repeat buyers are 72% of customers but 96.7% of revenue. The 1,455 Champions (25% of customers) bring in 69.9% of revenue.
5. **£1.45 million of past revenue is slipping away.** 826 At risk customers used to order about five times each but haven't ordered for a year on average. The ten most valuable include one UK customer with 155 past orders and no order for 373 days.

## Recommendations

1. Make customer ID mandatory at checkout, or match guest orders to accounts, so the unknown 13.5% can be analysed.
2. Start a win-back campaign for the At risk segment, beginning with the ten most valuable (query Q59).
3. Give the biggest customer in each international market an account manager, to reduce concentration risk.
4. Plan stock, staff and marketing around the September to November peak.
5. Investigate April 2011 with the sales team.

## Data notes

- Revenue is net: product lines with a price above zero, with cancellations subtracted. Postage, fees and manual adjustments are excluded.
- 15.5% of invoices are cancellations, but only 3.7% of sales value; two cancelled orders make up a third of that.
- 11,005 sets of identical duplicate lines were kept, because they may be genuine repeat scans.
- December 2011 contains only nine days and is excluded from growth comparisons.
